# C5 v1: Factory-built power blocks for AI data halls

## What is sold
A pre-assembled, pre-tested power block that replaces field electrical construction in an AI data center. One block conditions 5 MW: medium-voltage switchgear, transformer or rectifier, low-voltage or 800 V DC distribution, battery buffer, protection and controls, in a shippable enclosure. It is built and tested in a factory, set on site and commissioned in about 10 days, against 12 or more weeks of field work by electricians.

## Standard unit
One megawatt of conditioned power delivered to the data hall. Standard block: 5 MW.

## Price and contract
- $1.6M per MW (founder's assumption, to be tested against stick-built cost).
- 30% deposit at order, 50% at factory acceptance test, 20% at site acceptance.
- Delivery 26 weeks from order. Liquidated damages for late delivery of 0.5% of price per week, capped at 10%.
- Five-year warranty and remote monitoring.

## Founder's cost assumptions
Gross margin 25-30% at volume. A 100 MW order is $160M of revenue. First factory $30-40M including test bays.

## Why a customer buys today
Research notes say electrical work is 45-70% of data center build cost, prefabrication cuts electrician hours about 85%, one month saved is worth about $500,000 per MW, and electricians are scarce. Incumbent switchgear is booked through 2028. Nvidia's 800 V DC architecture resets designs in 2027-2028, so approved vendor lists reopen.

## Growth plan
Own the standard block for the 800 V DC era, then add cooling skids, then whole prefabricated halls, then service contracts on the installed base. Target: a new Vertiv.

## Known weak points the founder wants tested
- Breakers, transformers and switchgear come from the incumbents who also sell competing skids. Will they supply?
- 80+ vendors already sell prefab. Eaton bought Fibrebond for $1.43bn.
- UL listing, utility and inspector acceptance, hyperscaler qualification time.
- Is this a product or custom engineering per customer?
- What is left when the shortage ends?
