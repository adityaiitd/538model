# C2 v1: Risk capital for AI infrastructure (specialty reinsurer funded by capital markets)

## What is sold
Insurance capacity that AI data center projects cannot find today. First product: excess layers of construction all-risk with delay-in-startup, and operating property with business interruption, for campuses with $5-30bn of insurable value. Research notes say typical limits are $1.5-3.5bn against $10-30bn of value, and that only a third to a half of campus value is insured. Second product, later: residual value and offtake-default cover for lenders to GPU fleets.

## Standard unit
$1 million of limit for one year, in standard $100-250M layers that attach above the existing broker facilities (Aon, Marsh).

## Structure
- A Bermuda collateralized reinsurer, funded by institutional investors (insurance-linked securities funds, pensions, private credit). Target first close $300M of risk capital.
- An underwriting company with delegated authority, using A-rated fronting paper, distributing through the large broker facilities.
- Loss control as part of the product: schedule and site telemetry, AI-based monitoring of construction progress and commissioning, lithium battery and liquid-cooling risk engineering.

## Price
Rate on line of 1.5-3.0% for excess property and delay-in-startup layers (founder's assumption, to be tested). On $300M of collateral writing $1.5bn of limit, premium is $25-45M a year. The underwriting company earns a 12% fee on premium plus 15% of underwriting profit. Recognized revenue is earned premium in the reinsurer plus fees.

## Delivery
Licence and fronting in 6-9 months. First bound layer 9-12 months from start.

## Why a customer buys today
Lenders require delay-in-startup cover to close project financings. Projects grew from $1-2.5bn to $5-25bn in two years and capacity did not. Nvidia is asking insurers to share residual value risk. Insurers are short of capacity, not customers.

## Growth plan
Add residual value cover, offtake-default cover (credit wrap for non-investment-grade compute tenants, where spreads are SOFR+450-550 against SOFR+225 with an investment-grade offtaker), uptime cover, then power-project cover. Target: the main risk carrier for the AI and energy buildout.

## Known weak points the founder wants tested
- Is capacity really unavailable, or just expensive? Will insureds pay more, or keep self-insuring?
- Accumulation: one fire or one tornado at one campus could consume the whole vehicle.
- No loss history for residual value. Correlation of all GPU-backed risks.
- Why would brokers and fronting carriers give a new team the pen?
- Does this ever earn $100M of revenue in 24 months without irresponsible leverage?
