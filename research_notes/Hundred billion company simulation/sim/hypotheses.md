# Hypothesis screen: 31 businesses considered, five taken to simulation

Evidence references point to the research notes in the parent folder. Figures are as of November 2026.

## Taken to simulation

| # | Business | Why it passed the screen |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | Flexible bridge power for AI campuses: owned batteries that let a campus energize years early on interruptible grid service. Unit: firm MW-month. | Buyers have budgets. PJM cleared at its cap three times and is 6.8 GW short. Turbines are sold to 2030-32. Batteries are still buyable. Contract norms exist (Solaris, Liberty, VoltaGrid). |
| C2 | Risk capital for AI infrastructure: a capital-markets-funded reinsurer for construction, delay and residual value risk. Unit: $1M of limit-year. | Campuses carry $10-30bn of value against $1.5-3.5bn limits. Nvidia is asking insurers to share residual risk. Fits the founder's capital-markets skills. |
| C3 | AI-run healthcare billing office paid a share of collections. Unit: claim taken to cash. | Existing budget of 4-8% of collections. Waystar earns 43% margins. Buyers want outcome pricing and few vendors offer it. |
| C4 | Western tungsten conversion, first plant of a minor-metals refining platform. Unit: kg of tungsten. | Rotterdam price 3.7x China. US defense sourcing rule starts 1 Jan 2027. Limit is conversion capacity, not ore. |
| C5 | Factory-built power blocks for AI data halls. Unit: MW of conditioned power. | Electrical work is 45-70% of build cost. Prefab cuts electrician hours about 85%. The 800 V DC change reopens vendor lists. Vertiv and Eaton show the size of the prize. |

## Rejected at the screen

| # | Business | Reason for rejection |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Own behind-the-meter gas power and rent MW | Demand is proven, but engines ordered now arrive in 2028 and turbines in 2030-32. $50M buys about 35 MW. Winners already had fleets and OEM allocation. Folded into C1 as a later step. |
| 7 | Neocloud or GPU rental | Needs billions. CoreWeave's interest ($846M) exceeds adjusted operating income ($379M). Its bonds trade near 15%. Nscale lost $1.02bn in a half on $141M of revenue. |
| 8 | Open-model inference platform | Fireworks and Together reached about $1bn annualized, but gross margin is 45-50% while GPU rents rose and token prices fell 43% in ten weeks. No control over either side. |
| 9 | Compute futures exchange or GPU price index | Five index providers and six venues already chase a market with no confirmed volume. |
| 10 | GPU collateral monitoring and valuation data for lenders | Real need, small fee pool, slow adoption. Kept as a feature of C2. |
| 11 | Transformer manufacturing | 128-160 week lead times are real, but a new plant qualifies after incumbent capacity arrives in 2027-28, and grain-oriented steel is a second bottleneck. |
| 12 | Solid-state transformers or 800 V DC components | Needs a product breakthrough the founder does not hold. No UL-certified unit for data centers as of May 2026. Heron has $140M and a head start. |
| 13 | Refurbished turbines and long-lead equipment brokerage | One-time arbitrage on a finite pool of used cores. Disappears when OEM capacity arrives. |
| 14 | Data center commissioning and delivery services | Labor-heavy service with concentrated, financing-exposed customers. Fails the product test. |
| 15 | Expert data and RL environments for AI labs | Fastest proven ramp (Mercor $2bn gross run-rate, Fleet $1M to $60M in a year). But about 91% of Mercor's revenue comes from two buyers on quarter-length contracts, 35+ suppliers compete, and labs build in-house. No compounding advantage. |
| 16 | Agent sandbox and execution infrastructure | Daytona went $1M to $100M in four quarters, but revenue is a derivative of Claude Code and Codex volume, and labs can bundle execution. |
| 17 | AI customer support by resolution | Price already fell to $0.99-1.50 per resolution. Zendesk and HubSpot price the same way. |
| 18 | AI-native accounting firm or roll-up | Thrive Holdings ($2bn raised, 70+ firms, OpenAI as shareholder) and Long Lake are ahead. Audit stays licensed and out of reach. |
| 19 | AI-native law firm | Arizona is the only open state. California, Colorado and Illinois passed fee-sharing bans. Depends on regulatory change. |
| 20 | AI liability insurance or agent performance warranties | Limits are $2-50M and no premium volume is disclosed. Customers have not formed the buying habit. |
| 21 | Stablecoin cross-border payments or bank issuance tooling | Supply was flat in 2026. Dollar corridors earn 1-15bp. Circle, Stripe and Mastercard bundle it. BVNK sold for $1.8bn on $30bn of volume. |
| 22 | Agent payment rails | Genuinely agentic volume is $5,000-11,000 a month. No market yet. |
| 23 | Prediction market or new trading venue | Kalshi holds 81% US share at about $5bn revenue. Liquidity network effects favor the leader. Regulatory exposure. |
| 24 | Robot labor sold by output | Largest long-run prize, but humanoid sales are tiny (Agility $1.78M in 2025), utilization must exceed about 2,700 hours a year, and each site needs engineering. No path to $100M in two years. |
| 25 | Munitions sub-tier parts and energetics | Real shortage and $31.8bn of expansion money. Qualification takes 18-24 months, the buyer is one government, and the founder has no clearance or relationships. |
| 26 | Attritable drones or components | Airframes sell for about $2,000. Winners integrate vertically. No guaranteed quantities. |
| 27 | Fill-finish or biologics contract manufacturing | BIOSECURE bans cannot start before about 2028 and WuXi left the Pentagon list. Moving a marketed product takes 2-8 years. Resilience closed 6 of 10 plants after raising $2.25bn. |
| 28 | AI drug discovery or autonomous labs | No AI-designed drug has passed Phase 3. Recursion earns $18.6M a quarter. Depends on unproven science. |
| 29 | Cash-pay consumer health on rented infrastructure | Medvi made $401M in 2025 with two employees, then drew an FDA warning. Hims' gross margin fell from 74% to 57%. No moat. |
| 30 | AI-run early-phase clinical trial sites | US Phase 1 takes 17+ months against 7 in China, so the need is real. But sponsors take years to trust a new operator and no AI-native CRO discloses revenue. |
| 31 | Space-based compute | No revenue. Depends on undemonstrated economics. |
