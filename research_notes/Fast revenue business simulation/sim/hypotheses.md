# Hypothesis screen for the lower bar: $40M of gross revenue in 12-18 months, $100M within 36 months

27 businesses considered. Five taken to simulation. One carried over from the first study as a benchmark. Evidence references point to the research notes in the parent folder and in the first study's folder.

## Taken to simulation

| # | Business | Entry | Why it passed the screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Real-work data and long-horizon quality control for AI labs, licensed from enterprises | Organic | Supplying labs is the model with the most verified hits at this ramp on small capital: AfterQuery, micro1, Fleet. Buyers report shortage of real work traces and long-horizon quality control. |
| F2 | Trucked gas as bridge fuel for onsite power | Organic | Certarus booked $128.9M of revenue in one quarter of 2026. One 35-50 MW bridge contract is about $40M a year. Customers advance cash for equipment. |
| F3 | Customs duty recovery paid on results | Organic | Duties run about four times the 2024 level. Contingency pricing shortens the sale. Existing start-ups are small. |
| F4 | Hospital payment recovery, entered by buying a small firm | Acquisition | Proven at scale: Cloudmed sold for $4.1bn. Buyers in the first study signed contingency denial work readily. |
| F5 | Physical-work data for robotics labs | Organic | Robot-model companies are spending heavily on data and no supplier is large yet. |
| Benchmark | Onsite power entered by buying a generator fleet (first study, C1 v3) | Acquisition | Base model gives $40M in year one and $141M in year three, but panels put the chance of closing a purchase at 15-18%. Not re-simulated. |

## Rejected at the screen

| # | Business | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Self-serve AI application | Hits the ramp (Lovable, Midjourney) but needs product virality, not enterprise sales, and stalls when the model owner ships the same thing (Jasper). |
| 7 | Consumer cash-pay health on rented infrastructure | Medvi made $401M in 2025 with two people, then drew an FDA warning letter. Depends on regulatory tolerance. |
| 8 | Trading front-end on outsourced rails | Axiom took $100M of fees in under four months. Volume is speculative and fading. Poor founder fit. |
| 9 | Stocking and reselling long-lead electrical equipment | Padmount lead times fell to 20-65 weeks from over 100. New plants ship in 2027-28. No evidence slots can be resold. |
| 10 | Leasing GPUs and equipment to weak-credit AI operators | $40M of lease revenue needs about $105-110M of assets and $32-54M of equity. Adverse selection and no repossession precedent. |
| 11 | Reselling AI servers | Reaches the gross number on two deals, at single-digit margins. Fails the gross-margin screen. |
| 12 | Retail power for large loads | 190 MW served is $100M of gross revenue at a 2-5% margin, with collateral calls in a price spike. Fails the gross-margin screen. |
| 13 | Buying tariff refund claims | $128.4bn of about $166bn was certified for refund by November 2026 and filing is a free upload. The trade is over. |
| 14 | Payer-side payment integrity | 25-50 buyers matter and implementations are long. Cotiviti's top ten clients are 59% of revenue. |
| 15 | Debt collection roll-up with AI agents | Reaches revenue by acquisition. Reputational and state-licensing load, and no precedent found at this ramp. |
| 16 | Tax credit and refund recovery | Innovation Refunds cut over 40% of staff days after an IRS moratorium. One rule change ends it. |
| 17 | Transferable tax credit trading as principal | Gross revenue with a 1-2% margin. Fails the gross-margin screen. |
| 18 | IT-services or accounting roll-up | No case found that hit the ramp on under $20M of equity. Funded roll-ups are ahead. |
| 19 | Environment builder for labs, custom and lab-owned | Fleet did it, but 35+ suppliers now compete and buyers call many environments useless. F1 targets the part buyers say is short. |
| 20 | General expert marketplace for labs | Mercor, micro1, Handshake and Surge hold it at $1bn+ each. |
| 21 | Factory-built power blocks (first study, C5) | First unit takes 14-18 months. Best simulated order was 10 MW, $16M. |
| 22 | Islanded gas power from scratch (first study, C1 v2) | 18-24 months to a first block and $30-45M before revenue. |
| 23 | AI billing office (first study, C3) | Panels expected $3-8M run-rate at month 24. |
| 24 | Adviser assistant (first study, C7) | Panels expected $1.8M in year two. A lab shipped the product. |
| 25 | Critical-metals merchant with toll conversion | Converters use their own capacity. Scrap discount closes when an export order lapses in 2027. |
| 26 | Drone components for defense programs | Qualification and government buying cycles do not fit 18 months for a founder without clearances. |
| 27 | Freight brokerage with AI agents | Gross revenue comes fast at a 12-15% margin in a soft market. Large brokers are automating themselves. |
