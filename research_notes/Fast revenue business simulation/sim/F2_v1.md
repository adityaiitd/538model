# F2 v1: Bridge fuel for onsite power, delivered by truck (organic)

## What is sold
Natural gas delivered by truck to islanded gas power plants at AI data center sites that are waiting for a pipeline lateral. After the pipeline arrives, on-site liquefied gas storage as a second fuel path for sites that need 99.99% availability.

## Standard unit
One MMBtu delivered to the site.

## Price and contract
- Commodity price plus $11-12 per MMBtu for compression, transport and decanting. Research notes derive $12.31 above commodity from Certarus's third-quarter 2026 filing.
- Take-or-pay, 12-30 months. Customer advances about 12% of contract value at signing to fund equipment (Stabilis received $25M on a roughly $200M contract).
- Standard block: fuel for 35 MW of engines. About 7,500 MMBtu a day, 17 large trailer loads a day, 45-60 trailers, one compression hub at a pipeline tap within 100 miles. Revenue about $40M a year.

## Founder's cost assumptions
- Trailers $0.5M each (unverified), so $25-30M. Compression and decant equipment $8-12M. Tractors leased, or third-party hazmat carriers.
- $35-45M per block, funded by the customer advance, 60% equipment debt, and $10-15M of equity.
- EBITDA margin about 28% (Certarus). Trailers move to the next site when the pipeline arrives.

## Why a customer buys today
Laterals take 9-15 months or more and some are blocked (one New Mexico lateral slipped from August 2026 to February 2027). Trucked fuel costs about $131/MWh against about $22 on pipeline gas, but a month of delay costs an operator with idle GPUs far more. Named precedents: Oracle ran a Utah site on trucked gas for over a year. Certarus signed a 135 MW contract worth over $300M across 2.5 years.

## Growth plan
Several blocks, then permanent backup-fuel storage contracts, then a redeployable fuel fleet serving every delayed site.

## Known weak points the founder wants tested
- Can a new buyer get trailers and compression equipment in under 12 months? Certarus has a record order delivering through 2028.
- Will a customer give a first-time operator a hazardous-materials fuel contract on which its whole site depends?
- Certarus has 919 trailers and Stabilis has liquefaction. What stops them taking every contract?
- Every deal found is a bridge. What is the fleet worth when pipelines catch up?
- Drivers, hub permits, gas supply at the tap, winter.
