# F3 v1: Customs duty recovery paid on results (organic)

## What is sold
Recovery of refundable or overpaid US customs duties for importers and exporters.
1. Duty drawback: refund of duties on imported goods that were later exported or destroyed, with a five-year lookback.
2. Corrections: post-summary corrections and protests for classification, valuation and Section 232 metal-content errors, inside the 300-day window.
3. One-off: claims tied to the Section 122 tariffs of February to July 2026.

AI reads every entry line, bill of materials and export record to find and document claims. Licensed customs brokers review and file.

## Standard unit
One dollar refunded to the client.

## Price and contract
- 20% of refunds, no fee without a refund. Research notes give a market range of 10-30%.
- Option: the company advances 70% of the expected refund at filing for five extra points, funded by a credit line.
- Three-year engagement covering lookback and ongoing claims.

## Founder's assumptions
- US customs duties run $22-26bn a month in 2026, about four times the 2024 level. Section 301 duties and ordinary duties are drawback-eligible. Most Section 232 duties are not.
- $40M of fees needs $200M refunded: for example 60 clients averaging $3.3M.
- Sales are fast because the client pays nothing unless money comes back.
- First claim 4-8 months after signing, because each claimant needs its own drawback privilege and bond. Accelerated payment arrives 3-6 weeks after filing.
- Licence: hire licensed brokers as officers. File through a partner broker until the company's own licence is in place.
- Start-ups already in the field are small (seed rounds of about $5M).

## Known weak points the founder wants tested
- The verified drawback pool was about $1bn a year in 2019. Claims that 80% goes unclaimed come from vendors.
- Incumbent specialists and accounting firms hold the large exporters.
- The importer's current broker holds the entry data and the relationship.
- CBP processing capacity, audits, and penalties for wrong claims.
- Tariff policy can change the pool in either direction.
- Does anything accumulate, or is this a filing shop?
