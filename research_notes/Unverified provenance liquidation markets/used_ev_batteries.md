# Used EVs and used/second-life batteries (US and Europe): does unverifiable battery health depress prices? (as of 23 Nov 2026)

Reading notes for the report writer:
- "Snippet-level" means the figure came from a search-result summary of the linked page and the page itself was not opened. Treat those as less firm than items marked "opened".
- 27 search/fetch calls were used. Several key questions (warranty pricing, auction fee tables, licensing timelines, used grid-module prices) returned thin results; these are listed under Gaps rather than filled from memory.
- Headline finding: the measured price lift from a battery health certificate is 0% to about 4% in every transaction-based study found. The large depreciation gap versus petrol cars is attributed by lessors, T&E, Cox and iSeeCars mainly to new-car price cuts, incentives and policy, not to unverifiable battery condition. In the US in late 2026 used EVs sell faster than petrol cars and list at a premium to them.

## 1. Size: used EV sales, off-lease volume, wholesale volume, second-life battery market

### Takeaway
The US used EV market is running at about 48,000 to 49,000 retail units a month (3.3% of used sales) at an average listing price near $36,600, and EV lease returns rise from roughly 123,000 in 2025 to 215,000-300,000+ in 2026, about 600,000 in 2027 and a peak near 800,000 in 2028. In Europe (ex-UK) used BEVs reached 11.5% of used car sales by May 2026. Second-life battery market estimates are small and inconsistent ($0.1bn to $2.3bn for 2026).

## 2. The discount: depreciation versus petrol, residual-value losses, and attribution to battery uncertainty

### Takeaway
EVs lose far more value from original list price than petrol cars (57.2% versus 41.8% over five years in the US), and lessors on both sides of the Atlantic are booking or expecting losses. But the sources that decompose the gap attribute most of it to falling new-car prices, discounts and subsidies. In late 2026 the US used EV market is tight, not distressed: used EVs list above petrol cars and turn faster.

### Cited Findings

- iSeeCars 2026 study of over 950,000 five-year-old cars sold March 2025 to February 2026: EVs depreciated 57.2% over five years versus 41.8% for the overall market; hybrids 35.4%, trucks 34.2%; EV depreciation peaked at 67.1% in 2019; Nissan Leaf 63.1%, Tesla Model S 62% (snippet-level) — [Jalopnik](https://www.jalopnik.com/2194443/which-2021-ev-has-depreciated-most-over-five-years/)
- iSeeCars September 2026 analysis of over 1.5 million used cars: average used EV price up 9.8% year on year to $32,533, petrol up 0.3% to $31,954; used EVs sold about 13 days faster than comparable petrol models (snippet-level). Conflicts in direction with Cox's October figure of used EV listing prices down 1.9% year on year (different samples) — [Motor1](https://www.motor1.com/news/814090/used-evs-more-expensive-than-gas-cars-september-2026/); [AutoGuide](https://www.autoguide.com/auto/used-evs-now-sell-faster-than-gas-cars-44641342)
- October 2026 used EV days' supply 38 versus 47 for ICE+; Tesla 32 days (opened) — [Cox Automotive EV Market Monitor, October 2026](https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights/ev-market-monitor-october-2026/)

- BCA (Europe's largest remarketer): certified EVs achieved about 1.4% higher resale value and sold 2.7 days faster on average (opened, as cited by T&E) — [T&E via CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/30/what-is-the-situation-with-residual-values-in-europe/)
- Volteras six-month auction trial on Tesla Model 3s: prices were identical with or without a certificate; the benefit was a faster sale (snippet-level) — [Fleet News](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/trial-shows-ev-remarketing-boost-from-battery-certificates)
- Generational (UK certificate vendor): certified used EVs sold in 43.1 days versus 50.4 without, a 7.3-day gain over six months of data; no price figure (snippet-level) — [SMMT](https://www.smmt.co.uk/used-evs-sell-a-week-quicker-with-battery-health-certification-says-generational/)
- Aviloo survey of 913 respondents in seven countries: buyers say they would pay EUR 550 to EUR 1,100 more for a certified used EV, and certified cars sell "up to 36% faster" (snippet-level; stated preference from a vendor, not transaction data) — [Aviloo white paper, Sept 2025](https://aviloo.com/files/Aviloo/business-partner-area/AVILOO_Case Studies_White Papers/202509_AVILOO_White Paper_Remarketing_en.pdf)

### Inferences
- Every transaction-based measurement of certificate value found is in the range 0% to 4% of price, plus 3 to 7 days faster sale. On a $30,000 car that is $0 to $1,200. Nothing found supports "resell at a much higher price" from verification alone.
- The EV depreciation gap is real but is a loss already taken by the first owner or lessor against list price. The used buyer today is not obviously getting a distressed price: US used EVs list 6.5% above ICE and turn 9 days faster.
- The pain is concentrated at lessors, captives and rental firms who set residuals too high. Their problem is forecast error against new-car price cuts, which a battery report does not fix.

### Gaps
- No Ally Financial, GM Financial, Ford Credit or Ayvens figure for realised EV residual losses in 2025-2026 was found. The $8bn figure is a press extrapolation.
- No independent academic study of certificate price effects was found; all are vendor or remarketer data.
- No Manheim wholesale EV price series (EV versus ICE retention) was retrieved.
- No study was found that statistically splits the EV discount into battery-uncertainty versus new-price components. T&E's adjustment is the nearest.

## 3. Verification today: tools, accuracy, cost, and disclosure rules

### Takeaway
Battery state-of-health reporting is already embedded in the main wholesale channels (Manheim in the US, UK and Australia; ADESA with Recurrent; Copart UK and Manheim UK with Aviloo; BCA; Arval) and in Manheim's MMR valuations since October 2025. Regulation is about to make on-board state of health readable by anyone: US rules require an operator-accessible display from model year 2027 and the EU battery passport applies from 18 February 2027.

### Cited Findings
Tools and deployment
- Manheim provides a VIN-specific battery health score (0 to 100%) with estimated range; the same data is shown on Autotrader and Kelley Blue Book (snippet-level) — [Manheim](https://site.manheim.com/news/manheims-vin-specific-ev-battery-health-data-is-now-available-on-autotrader-and-kelley-blue-book)
- Since 28 October 2025 Manheim's MMR wholesale valuation includes a battery health score adjustment for eligible EVs, shown in the MMR tool (opened; no per-point dollar adjustment disclosed) — [Manheim](https://site.manheim.com/news/ev-battery-health-is-now-included-in-mmr)
- Cox piloted a mobile EV battery health tool at Manheim sites and ran EV battery testing at 10 Manheim locations (snippet-level) — [Cox Automotive](https://www.coxautoinc.com/news/cox-automotive-pilots-new-mobile-ev-battery-health-tool-at-manheim-sites-nationwide); [Manheim](https://site.manheim.com/news/ev-battery-health-testing-at-10-manheim-locations)
- Manheim UK sells an "EV Battery Health Certificate powered by AVILOO" (snippet-level) — [Manheim UK](https://www.manheim.co.uk/batteryhealth)
- ADESA US added battery health scoring for wholesale EVs in September 2023, built with Recurrent and AutoIMS (snippet-level; dated 2023) — [ADESA press release](https://www.adesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/092523_ADESA-US_Battery-Health-Scoring-Update_Press-Release_FINAL.pdf)
- Copart UK introduced an EV Battery Health Report with Aviloo in July 2026 (snippet-level) — [Copart UK](https://remarketing.copart.co.uk/2026/07/15/ev-battery-health-report/)
- Arval has issued over 30,000 battery health certificates across the UK and EU (opened, as cited by T&E) — [T&E via CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/30/what-is-the-situation-with-residual-values-in-europe/)
- Ayvens certifies battery health on used EVs it sells; its German analysis of about 7,300 returned leases found 95.5% battery capacity after 55,000 km (snippet-level) — [Vision Mobility](https://vision-mobility.de/en/news/used-evs-ayvens-determines-95-5-battery-health-after-55-000-kilometers-395295.html)
- Recurrent draws on more than 1 billion miles of driving data across tens of thousands of vehicles over six years (opened) — [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/features/794674/used-ev-battery-health-guide-2026/)
- Recurrent's CEO said its telematics approach covers 45 EV models with 10,000+ connected cars and is "really good on common EVs in common locations" (snippet-level; article date unknown, likely older) — [Canary Media](https://canarymedia.com/articles/batteries/the-used-ev-market-cant-thrive-without-accurate-battery-health-data)

What the tools find (how much hidden variance exists)
- Recurrent: the average EV retains 97% of original range after 3 years and 95% after 5; several brands show no observable range loss after 3 years (partly because of hidden buffers); the battery replacement rate among modern EVs is 0.3%; variance in battery health for EVs a few years old is "fairly tight" (opened) — [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/features/794674/used-ev-battery-health-guide-2026/)
- Recharged (used-EV retailer) reports 94% average remaining capacity across its sales in the past 12 months (snippet-level; company-reported) — [Recharged](https://recharged.com/articles/ev-sales-statistics-2026)

Rules
- US: 40 CFR 86.1815-27 (EPA's adoption of UN GTR 22 concepts) requires an operator-accessible display of State of Certified Energy with in-use accuracy within 5%; the State of Certified Range requirement was dropped (snippet-level) — [Cornell LII, 40 CFR 86.1815-27](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/40/86.1815-27)
- California ACC II requires a driver-accessible battery state-of-health display from model year 2026; CARB issued an enforcement-discretion letter in January 2025 covering 2026 and 2027 model years (snippet-level) — [CARB letter](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2025-02/ACC%20II%20Letter%20to%20Alliance_Signed.pdf); [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/696065/ev-diagnostic-system-arriving-by-2026/)
- InsideEVs reports that Congress revoked California's EPA waivers, nullifying ACC II; this conflicts with CARB's continuing rulemaking, so ACC II's legal status is unresolved in these notes (snippet-level) — [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/features/789263/battery-used-ev-trials-hardship/)
- ACC II durability and warranty figures as summarised by a third party: warranty to 70% state of health for 8 years/100,000 miles; durability 80% at 10 years/150,000 miles (snippet-level; not checked against CARB text) — [FleetRabbit](https://fleetrabbit.com/article/ev-fleet-battery-degradation-tracking)
- EU: the battery passport for EV batteries applies from 18 February 2027 and is to carry state-of-health data updated through the battery's life; access rules for third parties (independent repairers, used dealers) and several secondary acts are unfinished; Commission guidance that missed its February 2025 deadline was due 26 July 2026 (snippet-level) — [CMS Law](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/eu-sustainable-batteries-regulation-where-are-we-now); [Automobile Propre](https://www.automobile-propre.com/articles/batteries-des-voitures-electriques-leurope-impose-une-nouvelle-regle-pour-2027-mais-des-obstacles-persistent/); [Euro Weekly News](https://euroweeklynews.com/2026/05/30/europe-is-about-to-make-electric-car-batteries-far-less-mysterious-under-new-eu-rules/)
- China set an EV battery health (SOCE) accuracy rule in 2026 (snippet-level) — [Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/322783/20260803/china-sets-ev-battery-health-accuracy-rule-what-soce-means-used-car-buyers.htm)
- T&E recommends mandatory battery health disclosure at resale and standardised certification across OEMs, building on the passport and Euro 7 durability rules (opened; a recommendation, not law) — [T&E via CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/30/what-is-the-situation-with-residual-values-in-europe/)

### Inferences
- Verification is being commoditised from two directions: auction houses bundle a score into the listing and the valuation, and regulators are forcing the car itself to display a certified figure. An independent inspector's scan competes with a free number on the dashboard for model year 2027+ cars.
- Because 3 to 5 year-old packs cluster at 94% to 97% capacity with 0.3% replacement, there is little hidden bad news to find and little hidden good news to reveal in the off-lease cohort. The spread of outcomes, and therefore the value of verification, is larger in older, out-of-warranty cars (pre-2019 Leaf-type vehicles), which is a smaller and cheaper segment.
- The passport's data-access rules for independent third parties are still open, which is both a risk (OEM gatekeeping) and the one place a specialist could have an edge.

### Gaps
- No verified accuracy, price per test, or test duration was retrieved for Aviloo, Recurrent, Twaice, Voltest, Moba or Generational. Not searched individually.
- No evidence was found of a Carfax or AutoCheck battery-health product.
- No source directly tested whether a mobile OBD scan alone is sufficient versus a full charge/discharge test.
- The elevenes.com page lists a two-year postponement to 18 August 2027 under Regulation (EU) 2025/1561; my understanding is that this postponement concerns the battery due-diligence obligations and not the passport, but this was not checked against the Official Journal.
- Euro 7 battery durability requirements were not retrieved.

## 4. Warranty: extended battery warranties and residual-value guarantees

### Takeaway
Named providers exist (Xcelerate in the US, Altelium backed by Lloyd's in the UK, Munich Re as a B2B capacity provider, OEM certified pre-owned schemes), and OEM battery warranties of 8 years/100,000 miles or 160,000 km already cover most off-lease cars. No pricing or loss experience was found.

### Cited Findings
- Xcelerate (US) sells third-party EV service contracts including an "XCare Battery & Drive Unit" plan, with deductibles from $0, sold online; it announced a full battery-replacement warranty in December 2022 (snippet-level; dated 2022-2023) — [CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2022/12/30/xcelerate-to-offer-electric-vehicle-owners-worlds-first-full-battery-replacement-warranty/); [ConsumerAffairs](https://www.consumeraffairs.com/automotive/ev-extended-warranty-cost.html)
- Altelium (Lancaster University spin-out) offers insurance-backed warranties for first- and second-life batteries, backed by Lloyd's of London (snippet-level) — [Lancaster University](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/sci-tech/research/impact/recharging-the-battery-market); [Faraday Institution](https://faraday.ac.uk/success-stories/developing-diagnostic-models-to-warranty-batteries/)
- Munich Re offers an "E-Mobility Warranty Cover" that lets clients offer battery warranties to their own customers (B2B, no consumer price) (snippet-level) — [Munich Re](https://www.munichre.com/en/solutions/for-industry-clients/enabling-the-e-mobility-transition.html)
- Battery replacement coverage limits in the market typically range from $5,000 to $25,000 (snippet-level; generic, from a retailer's article) — [Recharged](https://recharged.com/articles/ev-battery-replacement-insurance/)
- EU norm for OEM battery warranty is about 8 years or 160,000 km; BYD Certified Pre-Owned guarantees a battery health score of 90, with a 1-year/20,000 km vehicle warranty and the remainder of the 8-year/200,000 km battery warranty (opened) — [T&E via CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/30/what-is-the-situation-with-residual-values-in-europe/)
- Residual-value guarantee precedent: Tesla's European buyback of early Model S/X at at least 50% of base purchase price after 3 years; T&E proposes time-limited RV guarantee schemes for fleets (opened) — [T&E via CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/30/what-is-the-situation-with-residual-values-in-europe/)
- UK insurers appear "more inclined to write off any EV with battery damage, rather than attempt a repair", and lessors report rising insurance premiums (opened, page 12) — [BVRLA Leasing Outlook, October 2026](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/media/1/root/bvrla-lor-oct26-final.pdf)

### Inferences
- A 2 to 4 year-old off-lease EV still has 4 to 6 years of OEM battery warranty, so an intermediary's added battery warranty has low incremental value to the buyer until the car is 8+ years old.
- With a 0.3% replacement rate, expected battery claims cost is low; the open underwriting question is tail cost per claim, not frequency.

### Gaps
- No price for any used-EV battery warranty was found. No insurer loss ratio or claims data was found.
- No current (2026) provider of residual-value insurance for used EVs was identified.
- Whether OEM battery warranties transfer cleanly to second owners by brand was not checked.

## 5. Who already does this: specialist dealers, marketplaces, wholesale platforms, OEM programmes

### Takeaway
The space is occupied at every layer: funded used-EV retailers in the US (Ever, Recharged, Plug), auction houses with built-in battery scoring (Manheim, ADESA, Copart UK, BCA), lessors certifying their own returns (Arval, Ayvens) and OEM certified programmes (BYD, Tesla). Funding rounds are modest ($14m to $31m), which suggests an early but contested market, not an empty one.

### Cited Findings
- Ever (San Francisco) raised a $31m Series A led by Eclipse, with Ibex Investors, Lifeline Ventures and JIMCO; describes itself as an "AI-native, full-stack auto retail business", digital-first with physical locations, EV-only for now; cites "thousands of customers" and a sales team "two to three times more productive"; units, revenue and margins not disclosed (opened) — [TechCrunch, 12 Feb 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/12/eclipse-backs-all-ev-marketplace-ever-in-31m-funding-round)
- Recharged: used-EV marketplace where every car carries a "Recharged Score" battery health report; CB Insights lists a $14.46m raise dated 11 December 2025 (snippet-level; aggregator figure, not confirmed) — [Recharged](https://recharged.com/articles/used-ev-market-trends-2026/); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/compare/ever-1393-vs-leaselot)
- Plug (EV wholesale marketplace) raised a $20m Series A led by Lightspeed (snippet-level; from a LinkedIn profile, date and details unverified) — [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gt3nc)
- Manheim, ADESA, Copart UK, BCA, Manheim UK: battery health scoring or certificates in the wholesale lane (see section 3 for sources) — [Manheim](https://site.manheim.com/news/ev-battery-health-is-now-included-in-mmr); [ADESA](https://www.adesa.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/092523_ADESA-US_Battery-Health-Scoring-Update_Press-Release_FINAL.pdf); [Copart UK](https://remarketing.copart.co.uk/2026/07/15/ev-battery-health-report/)

### Inferences
- The asset owners with the biggest losses (lessors) are solving disposal themselves by re-leasing and certifying in-house, which removes supply from any third-party intermediary.
- The largest single pool of US used EVs is Tesla, where the OEM holds the data and runs its own used programme. A third-party verifier is weakest exactly where volume is highest.

### Gaps
- No verified detail on Tesla's used/CPO programme terms, Carvana's or CarMax's EV battery disclosures, ACV's or OPENLANE's battery reports in 2026.
- ADESA's US physical auctions are, to my knowledge, owned by Carvana since 2022 while OPENLANE kept the digital business; this was not verified in this session.
- No European used-EV specialist startups (for example in Germany, France, the Netherlands or the Nordics) were identified with funding and traction. Not searched specifically.
- Ever's inventory model (own stock versus consignment) is not stated in the TechCrunch piece.

## 6. Business model norms: fees, gross margin, consignment, inventory

### Takeaway
Used-vehicle front-end gross at US franchised dealers is only about $1,250 to $1,650 per unit in 2026, so a 1% to 4% certificate uplift ($300 to $1,200 on a $30,000 car) is material relative to dealer gross but small in absolute terms. Auction-sourced cars earn about $1,200 less gross than non-auction cars.

### Cited Findings
- Q2 2026 used gross profit per unit at franchised dealers: about $1,409 in one dataset (down 10% year on year) and $1,634 in another (down 2%); Q1 2026: $1,253 used and $1,781 new (snippet-level; different samples) — [Dealership Guy](https://news.dealershipguy.com/p/gross-profit-per-unit-drops-for-top-retailers-in-q2); [Digital Dealer](https://digitaldealer.com/news/average-dealership-profit-is-down-but-the-floor-may-be-in-sight/172689/); [WardsAuto](https://www.wardsauto.com/news/fixed-ops-fi-carry-the-load-for-dealerships-in-q1/818466/)
- Sonic Automotive: non-auction-sourced vehicles earn around $1,200 more gross per unit than auction cars (snippet-level) — [WardsAuto](https://www.wardsauto.com/news/sonic-automotives-focus-on-used-cars-led-to-record-profits-in-q1/819501/)
- Dealer profit increasingly comes from finance and insurance; F&I profit per vehicle hit a record high (snippet-level, headline) — [Digital Dealer](https://digitaldealer.com/news/the-post-gross-era-why-dealership-profit-now-runs-through-the-finance-office/172683/)
- OPENLANE's buy fee scales with the vehicle's purchase price and includes title transfer and escrow; no dollar table retrieved (snippet-level) — [OPENLANE](https://www.openlane.com/buyers/)
- Manheim's Vehicle Availability Policy (revised 1 July 2026) allows a seller fee of up to $500 for an unfulfilled simulcast or timed sale (snippet-level) — [Manheim](https://site.manheim.com/en/marketplace-policies/us-policies/ove-addendum-naaaarbitration-policy/ove-vehicle-availability-policy.html)
- UK used leasing economics: lessors say they must inspect, collect, refurbish and deliver used cars quickly and price the rental 10% to 40% below a new lease (opened, pages 3 and 8) — [BVRLA Leasing Outlook, October 2026](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/media/1/root/bvrla-lor-oct26-final.pdf)

### Inferences
- With front-end gross of about $1,400 a car, a verify-warrant-resell intermediary has to cover inspection, transport, reconditioning, warranty reserve and auction fees out of a spread that the certificate studies put at $0 to $1,200. The margin for a "much higher price" resale is not visible in the data.
- The observable way to earn more per unit is sourcing outside auction (Sonic's $1,200) and F&I, not certification.

### Gaps
- No US Manheim, OPENLANE or ACV buyer/seller fee schedule in dollars was found.
- No data on floorplan finance rates or advance rates for EVs, or on lenders discounting EV collateral, was found.
- No US consignment-model benchmark (fee percentage, legal treatment) was found.

## 7. Legal start: dealer licensing and whether a non-dealer can inspect, certify and broker

### Takeaway
Selling or brokering vehicles requires a state dealer or broker licence in the states checked; California lists used dealer, wholesale-only dealer and auto broker endorsement as distinct categories, with a commonly cited timeline of 6 to 8 weeks. Inspection and certification alone were not found to require a dealer licence, but this was not confirmed from a primary source.

### Cited Findings
- California DMV requires a dealer education certificate and a passed used-dealer test for used and wholesale-only dealer licences, and lists an auto broker endorsement as a separate item (snippet-level) — [California DMV](https://dmv.ca.gov/portal/vehicle-industry-services/occupational-licensing/occupational-licenses/vehicle-dealer-license)
- California surety bond: $50,000 for used dealers, $10,000 for wholesale-only dealers (snippet-level; from a 2025 ACV guide, check against DMV) — [ACV Auctions](https://www.acvauctions.com/blog/how-to-get-a-dealer-license-in-california)
- California used dealer licensing takes roughly 6 to 8 weeks (snippet-level; from a 2017 industry blog, approximate) — [GetMyAuto](https://dealers.getmyauto.com/blog/can-get-dealers-license)
- Texas: the independent (GDN) licence is the basic licence for buying, selling or exchanging used vehicles; each state runs its own process with no reciprocity (snippet-level) — [TxDMV](https://www.txdmv.gov/dealer-license-application-process)

### Inferences
- A multi-state intermediary that takes title or brokers sales needs a licence per state (or must work through licensed dealer partners), which adds months and bonding cost before the first sale. A pure inspection-and-report business avoids this, but then it is a certificate vendor competing with Aviloo, Recurrent and the auction houses.

### Gaps
- No processing times for Texas, Florida or other states. No primary-source answer on whether consignment or brokering without a dealer licence is permitted anywhere. No European licensing information was gathered.
- Wholesale auction access (Manheim, OPENLANE, ACV) generally requires a dealer licence; this is my understanding and was not verified in this session.

## 8. Evidence that the discount is NOT about verification

### Takeaway
The strongest evidence found points away from verification as the cause. New EV prices fell over 12% after the US federal credit ended on 30 September 2025, lessors and T&E name discounting and policy as the drivers, measured certificate uplift is 0% to 4%, and battery fleets are healthy and tightly clustered. In late 2026 US used EV prices are rising and turning faster than petrol cars.

### Cited Findings
- The US federal EV tax credits ended 30 September 2025 (opened; "the federal tax credit expired") — [Cox Automotive EV Market Monitor, October 2026](https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights/ev-market-monitor-october-2026/)
- New EV listing prices on Cars.com fell over 12% after the credit ended; another report puts sticker prices down 12.3% to $58,071 six months after expiry (snippet-level) — [Cars.com](https://cars.com/articles/new-evs-have-gotten-cheaper-since-the-federal-electric-vehicle-tax-credit-ended-523577/); [GCN](https://gcn.com/?p=23056)
- Hyundai cut 2026 Ioniq 5 sticker prices by an average of more than $9,000 (snippet-level) — [Carscoops](https://www.carscoops.com/2026/02/used-ev-resale-values/)
- iSeeCars (February 2026): after the credit ended, average used Tesla prices rose 4.3% to $31,329 while the rest of the used EV market fell 3.6% to $23,738; EV share of the lightly used market fell from 3.5% to 2.8% between September 2025 and January 2026, attributed mainly to loss of the used-EV credit (snippet-level) — [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/02/25/used-tesla-prices-rise-4-3-while-rest-of-ev-market-drops-after-tax-credit-ends); [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/news/787777/used-ev-prices-plummet-teslas-remain-resilient/)
- Analyst comment: "If we see a bunch of price cuts, especially on existing models, that will hurt residual values" (snippet-level) — [ArcaMax](https://www.arcamax.com/business/businessnews/s-3892304)
- Europe: recovery of used EV values is "at risk from new car discounts" (Indicata); used BEV prices up 2% since summer (snippet-level; year not confirmed) — [Fleet News](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/recovery-of-used-electric-vehicle-values-at-risk-from-new-car-discounts)
- UK lessors attribute residual-value weakness to OEM discounting, Chinese entrants, the ZEV Mandate and tax-policy uncertainty, and say used-car demand is "susceptible to negative media stories" (opened, pages 3, 10, 11) — [BVRLA Leasing Outlook, October 2026](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/media/1/root/bvrla-lor-oct26-final.pdf)
- T&E: adjusting for subsidies, taxes and current new prices shrinks the BEV-petrol residual gap from 12.9 points to 2.6 points, i.e. about 80% of the headline gap is explained by new-price effects (opened) — [T&E via CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/30/what-is-the-situation-with-residual-values-in-europe/)
- Battery fleets are healthy: 97% range at 3 years, 95% at 5 years, 0.3% replacement rate (Recurrent); 95.5% capacity at 55,000 km on about 7,300 Ayvens returns (opened / snippet-level) — [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/features/794674/used-ev-battery-health-guide-2026/); [Vision Mobility](https://vision-mobility.de/en/news/used-evs-ayvens-determines-95-5-battery-health-after-55-000-kilometers-395295.html)
- US used EVs in October 2026: 38 days' supply versus 47 for ICE+, listing premium of 6.5% over ICE+, sales up 73% year on year (opened) — [Cox Automotive EV Market Monitor, October 2026](https://www.coxautoinc.com/insights/ev-market-monitor-october-2026/)
- WardsAuto headline: "Gas prices might help offset glut of off-lease EVs" (headline only, not opened) — [WardsAuto](https://www.wardsauto.com/news/gas-prices-might-help-offset-glut-of-off-lease-evs/824912/)

Evidence FOR a verification effect (for balance)
- InsideEVs states that uncertainty about range loss is one factor depressing used EV values (opened; no number) — [InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/features/794674/used-ev-battery-health-guide-2026/)
- T&E lists "concerns about battery life" among causes and makes standardised battery certification its first policy recommendation (opened) — [T&E via CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/30/what-is-the-situation-with-residual-values-in-europe/)
- UK leasing executives report some private drivers still not considering BEVs "due to range and charging concerns" (opened, page 6) — [BVRLA Leasing Outlook, October 2026](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/media/1/root/bvrla-lor-oct26-final.pdf)
- Certificates do speed sales by 3 to 7 days and lift price 0% to 4% (see section 2) — [SMMT / Generational](https://www.smmt.co.uk/used-evs-sell-a-week-quicker-with-battery-health-certification-says-generational/)

### Inferences
- The used EV case fails the founder's test on two counts. First, assets are not selling at liquidation prices to the next buyer: the loss is against list price and sits with the first owner or lessor. Second, where verification has been tried at scale, the price moved by low single digits.
- The used price of an EV is anchored to the discounted price of a new one. Verification cannot lift a used car above that anchor.
- Where a verification wedge might still exist: older out-of-warranty EVs, non-Tesla models with thin data, cars with accident or battery-damage history (UK insurers write these off), cross-border European trade, and the forthcoming 2027-2028 auction surge if supply outruns dealer appetite. None of these was quantified.

### Gaps
- No evidence was found on price dispersion within a model at a given age/mileage, which would be the direct measure of how much hidden-condition discount exists.
- No data on salvage or battery-damaged EV pricing (Copart, IAA) was gathered, though the BVRLA write-off comment suggests a possible mispriced pool.

## 9. Stationary side: used or surplus grid battery modules and second-life packs

### Takeaway
Second-life stationary storage is a small market squeezed by the falling price of new LFP modules. Named operators (B2U, Redwood Energy, Moment Energy, Tricera) buy packs and integrate them into their own projects; no open resale marketplace or published discount for used grid modules was found.

### Cited Findings
- New LFP module prices have fallen sharply over the past two to three years, bringing new and second-life pricing close to parity and weakening the reuse case; B2U's CEO maintains US second-life BESS can compete with Chinese new-battery pricing (snippet-level) — [Energy-Storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/b2u-ceo-us-second-life-bess-can-compete-with-china-price/); [Energy-Storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/second-life-sector-still-needs-to-meet-performance-pricing-and-safety-challenges/)
- B2U's CEO claims second-life EV batteries cut grid storage costs in half (snippet-level, headline; company claim) — [Battery Technology](https://www.batterytechonline.com/stationary-batteries/second-life-ev-batteries-are-cutting-grid-storage-costs-in-half-says-b2u-ceo)
- Redwood Energy (launched 2025) built a 12 MW / 63 MWh microgrid from retired EV batteries powering a data centre near Reno, Nevada (snippet-level) — [Texas Tribune](https://www.texastribune.org/2026/02/24/texas-power-grid-auto-batteries-storage/)
- A Texas project turned 500 retired EV batteries into a 24 MWh grid buffer (snippet-level, headline) — [Autobody News](https://www.autobodynews.com/news/texas-project-turns-500-retired-ev-batteries-into-24-mwh-grid-buffer)
- Tricera (Germany) sources leftover modules when production lines stop; it notes buyers must design their own battery management system and that module sizes and chemistries vary (snippet-level) — [Energy-Storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/german-ev-industry-opens-up-second-use-battery-market-for-energy-storage)
- Pricing claims: new automotive-grade packs averaged $108/kWh in 2025; stationary developers buying used modules at $30 to $50/kWh reach parity with lead-acid (snippet-level; market-research report, unverified) — [Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6260398/ev-battery-reuse-market-share-analysis)
- An investor thesis page states used EV batteries are "30%+ cheaper" than new (snippet-level; investor summary, not a transaction price) — [Compound VC](https://compound.vc/thesis/thesis/2nd-life-batteries-for-storage)
- State-of-health uncertainty is cited as a key restraint on second-life market growth; economics depend on remaining useful life, integration cost, performance guarantees and recycling obligations (snippet-level; market-research summaries) — [MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/new-second-life-battery-opportunities-bess-market-250633441.html)
- Altelium provides Lloyd's-backed warranties for second-life battery storage (snippet-level) — [Lancaster University](https://www.lancaster.ac.uk/sci-tech/research/impact/recharging-the-battery-market)

### Inferences
- In stationary storage the verification problem is real (heterogeneous modules, no common BMS, state-of-health uncertainty), but the ceiling on resale price is the new-LFP price, which keeps falling. Verified second-life packs can at best sell at a discount to a cheap and improving new product.
- The existing players are vertically integrated project developers, not traders. That suggests margins come from integration and energy revenue, not from buy-verify-resell.

### Gaps
- No transaction prices for used grid-scale modules or decommissioned BESS containers were found. No marketplace or broker for surplus grid batteries was identified. No Moment Energy or B2U procurement pricing was found.
- BloombergNEF or IDTechEx second-life data sits behind paywalls and was not accessed.
