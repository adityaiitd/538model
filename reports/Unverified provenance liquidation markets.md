# Verification alone cures few liquidation discounts

Yes, there are large markets with large liquidation discounts, but in most of them the discount is not a verification discount that a newcomer can cure. Across the five sets of research notes the binding constraint is who is allowed to vouch for the asset, not the cost of measuring it. The original manufacturer, a code-stamped fabricator, an accredited vendor list or a secured lender holds that right in most of the eleven markets reviewed. Three markets fit in part. The best is used electrical gear that the 2026 electrical code allows to be reconditioned, and it works only if the firm becomes the reconditioner of record and carries the liability. On-site erasure and resale of data-center hardware is second, and consignment of excess electronic components is third. No source in any market isolates the share of the discount caused by missing paperwork alone, so the curable gap in each market is an inference. Charging more than a broker fee requires being the party whose name and insurance stand behind the asset.

## Three tests sort the eleven markets

A market fits the founder's plan only if it passes three tests. Test 1 asks whether doubt causes the discount, or whether the cause is a forced sale, real damage, new-product price cuts or oversupply. Test 2 asks whether records review plus a mobile inspection can cure the doubt, or whether the cure needs a lab, a court, elapsed time or a document that no longer exists. Test 3 asks whether a new firm can be the party that vouches, or whether that right is reserved. The scores are my reading of the notes. Sizes and discounts carry the notes' source flags.

## In most markets the discount has another cause

Used EVs show the pattern most clearly because the certificate effect has been measured. A battery health certificate lifted price by 2.4% on average at Manheim, with a range of 1% to 4% ([Cox](https://www.coxautoinc.com/news/cox-automotive-pilots-new-mobile-ev-battery-health-tool-at-manheim-sites-nationwide), 2023, search summary). The lift was 1.4% at BCA and 0% in a Volteras trial on Tesla Model 3s ([T&E via CleanTechnica](https://cleantechnica.com/2026/09/30/what-is-the-situation-with-residual-values-in-europe/), [Fleet News](https://www.fleetnews.co.uk/news/trial-shows-ev-remarketing-boost-from-battery-certificates)). T&E, an advocacy group, found the European gap between electric and petrol residual values shrank from 12.9 points to 2.6 points when measured against current new prices. US new EV list prices fell more than 12% after the federal credit ended ([Cars.com](https://cars.com/articles/new-evs-have-gotten-cheaper-since-the-federal-electric-vehicle-tax-credit-ended-523577/), search summary). Batteries keep 95% of range after five years with a 0.3% replacement rate ([Recurrent via InsideEVs](https://insideevs.com/features/794674/used-ev-battery-health-guide-2026/)), so there is little hidden condition to reveal. Second-life packs face the same ceiling because new LFP prices have fallen close to parity ([Energy-Storage.news](https://www.energy-storage.news/second-life-sector-still-needs-to-meet-performance-pricing-and-safety-challenges/)).

Retail returns are discounted for condition and handling. ReturnPro, a vendor, claims 60 cents on the dollar from graded resale against 12 cents from bulk sale, before the labour of testing and repacking ([ReturnPro](https://www.returnpro.com/resources/blog/retail-refurbishment-for-returns-2026)). The component figures use different bases, cost for the outright sale and market value for consignment, and the researchers read most of the gap as selling everything today against selling good lines over months. Foreclosure buyers paid 65.3% of estimated value against a lender reserve of 64.2%, so the reserve sets the price and not a title haircut (researchers' inference).

No source separates the paperwork-only share of the discount from damage, obsolescence, speed or oversupply. That gap applies to every market here except used EVs.

## Where doubt is the cause, the cure or the right to vouch is reserved

Aircraft parts pass test 1 and fail the other two. A missing disk sheet cannot be rebuilt by inspection, and recovery gets harder each year as shop record retention lapses ([ee-aero](https://ee-aero.com/aircraft-records/aircraft/cfm56-llp-trace-records-review/), vendor). Only a repair station, an air carrier or a production approval holder can issue a release certificate, and a distributor cannot ([Coalition report](https://www.aviationsuppliers.org/asa/files/cclibraryfiles/filename/000000005402/Aviation%20Supply%20Chain%20Integrity%20Coalition%20-%20Report%20-%20FINAL.pdf)). A fresh tag on a part with no life limit is ordinary work for every trader. AerSale earned a 31.5% gross margin and won about 6% of its feedstock bids in 2025 (search summaries: [results](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/593410/aersale-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results), [call](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/aersale-asle-q4-2025-earnings-224217503.html)), so the scarce input is aircraft to tear down. AOG Technics sold £6.9M of parts on forged certificates and caused more than £39.3M of losses ([SFO](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/sfo-secures-4-year-prison-sentence-for-aircraft-parts-fraud)). The industry response was to buy from accredited distributors.

Steel fails test 3 outright. The ASME rule says only a Certificate Holder, the vessel or part manufacturer, shall recertify material ([ASME text, unofficial copy](https://havacaran.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/ASME-Sec-VIII-Div1-2023.pdf)). Component counterfeit tests take three days to two weeks in a lab ([Micross](https://micross.com/products-and-services/counterfeit-mitigation), vendor), and 24% of suspect counterfeit parts reported in 2025 passed electrical testing ([ERAI](https://www.erai.com/erai_blog/3192/2025_annual_report)). Quiet title after a tax deed takes 3 to 18 months on law-firm and forum estimates ([DeWitt Law](https://dewittlaw.com/foreclosure/8104/quiet-title-actions-tax-deed-sale), [BiggerPockets](https://biggerpockets.com/forums/70/topics/683845-which-tax-deed-states-require-a-quiet-title-action)). Heirs' property, a stock of $32B to $41B, needs probate that ran six years in one case study ([NCLC](https://www.nclc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2026.09_Report_Clearing-the-Path-to-Title.pdf)). At Tricolor about 40% of roughly 70,000 loans shared vehicle numbers with another loan ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/tricolor-eyeing-sale-funding-pitches-while-fraud-probes-advance), search summary), and a Chapter 7 trustee and the secured lenders control the cure.

## Three partial fits, ranked

### 1. Used electrical gear that the code allows to be reconditioned

New switchgear lead times run from 44 weeks (Wood Mackenzie, Q2 2025) to 52-84 weeks or more for data-center-class gear ([POWER](https://powermag.com/transformers-in-2026-shortage-scramble-or-self-inflicted-crisis/), [GigaEnergy](https://www.gigaenergy.com/blog/lead-time-delays-for-data-centers)). A used power breaker sells for $1,200 to $9,000 ([Circuit Breaker Buyer USA](https://circuitbreakerbuyerusa.com/blog/how-to-sell-abb-breakers-data-center-decommissions-northern-virginia), vendor). NEMA's summary of the 2026 National Electrical Code says the code permits reconditioned power circuit breakers, switchgear, switchboards and electromechanical protective relays. It bars reconditioned molded-case breakers, panelboards, electronic trip units, transfer switches and busway ([NEMA CS 70011-2026](https://ieci.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NEMA-CS-70011-2026-Technical-Position-on-Reconditioned-Equipment-CS-100.pdf)). The code text itself was not read. The reconditioner must remove the original listing mark and mark the equipment with its own name, the date and the word "reconditioned". UL lets a third party become an authorised rebuilder after an investigation and under factory surveillance ([UL](https://ul.com/resources/faqs-about-safety-certification-reconditioned-equipment)), and PEARL accredits reconditioning shops ([PEARL](https://pearl1.org/about-accreditation/)).

It is only a partial fit. Part of the discount is real wear and unknown service history, which NEMA says can harm safety and reliability. Reconditioning needs a test shop, so a mobile inspection only screens which units to accept. Test 3 passes only if the firm or its shop partner is the reconditioner of record, which is a product-liability position and not a marketplace position. Manufacturers contest resale. Eaton sued Breakers Unlimited in January 2025 alleging altered breakers and sought a seizure order, and no ruling was found ([CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/69580945/eaton-corporation-v-breakers-unlimited-inc/)). The size of the US used electrical gear market is not sourced, which is a real gap. A shallow search found no venture-funded AI firm in the segment, and the researchers call that weak evidence.

This supports the earlier report's recommendation of an agent desk for surplus power and data-center electrical gear with a warranty wrap, and it changes two things. A pure agent cannot supply the name on the gear, so the desk needs a shop or shop partner that takes liability. The legal path is clearest for breakers and switchgear at four-figure tickets, which means smaller tickets and higher volume than the turbine packages in that report. NEMA also says transformers are not suitable for reconditioning. That is a trade-body position and not code text, and Maddox already warrants reconditioned transformers for three years ([Maddox](https://www.maddox.com/dev/faqs-v2)).

### 2. On-site verified erasure and resale of data-center hardware

This passes tests 1 and 2. In Blancco's vendor-commissioned survey 77% of organisations would prefer reuse if devices can be completely sanitised ([Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/e-scrap/2026/05/19/ai-data-anxiety-push-enterprises-to-destroy-working-devices-report/)). SK tes reports a 96% on-site wipe yield and $2.3M more than shredding on 72,000 drives ([SK tes](https://www.sktes.com/case-studies/maximizing-value-in-hyperscale-server-decommissioning), vendor case study), about $32 a drive on the researchers' arithmetic. The blocker is liability policy. The SEC fined Morgan Stanley $35M after unwiped drives were resold ([FT via Slashdot](https://hardware.slashdot.org/story/22/10/06/1030211/big-tech-banks-government-departments-shred-millions-of-storage-devices-they-could-reuse)). Liability is an insurance problem, which matches the founder's underwriting skill. That is an inference and not a sourced finding. The researchers' view is that a start-up balance sheet cannot credibly take that liability, and no source shows an insurer writes the cover. The field is crowded. Iron Mountain's disposal unit grew 88% in Q2 2026, expects to approach $1B of revenue this year and is buying smaller operators ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/iron-mountain-q2-earnings-call-highlights), [Resource Recycling](https://resource-recycling.com/e-scrap/2026/05/05/iron-mountain-puts-itad-at-the-center-of-its-growth/)). The 2026 shortage has also closed the used-price gap, with used server memory up 250-335% in a year on a reseller's appraisal page ([AM Compute](https://www.amcompute.com/appraisals/memory)).

### 3. Consignment of excess components and manufacturer surplus

The fivefold uplift on Component Sense's figures comes from patient selling of first-hand stock whose trace is intact. Component Sense has done this since 2001 and went two years without a sale ([Altium](https://resources.altium.com/p/why-factories-crush-perfectly-good-components)). Smith, with $2.60B of 2024 revenue, offers consignment too ([Smith](https://smithweb.com/services/supply-chain-solutions/excess-inventory-solutions/)). In industrial surplus Amplio raised an $11.1M Series A for AI appraisal and resale ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250924769724/en/Amplio-Secures-11.1M-Series-A-to-Scale-AI-Powered-Surplus-Solutions-for-Global-Manufacturers/)).

## Where to look next, and what a higher fee requires

The evidence supports one rule. A market fits when doubt causes the discount, the doubt can be measured on site, and the right to vouch can be acquired by taking liability. Electrical reconditioning and drive erasure meet the third condition because the rules name a role an outsider can take. Aviation, steel, tax deeds and loan pools do not, because the rules name someone else.

Asset-light intermediaries in surplus goods earn about 10% to 22% of sale price. Liquidity Services' government and industrial segments work out to 9.8% and 18.6% of gross merchandise volume on the researchers' arithmetic, and RB Global reports 21.6% ([Liquidity Services](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001235468/000119312526470517/lqdt-ex99_1.htm), [RB Global](https://investor.rbglobal.com/news/news-details/2026/RB-Global-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx)). Charging more requires becoming the party whose name and insurance stand behind the asset: the reconditioner marked on the breaker, the firm that certifies the erasure, the dealer that warrants the rebuilt transformer. AI on records and photos lowers the cost of deciding which units to accept. It does not supply the name. Both points are my reading of the notes, not sourced findings.

## A seven-day test for reconditioned breakers and switchgear

The test costs no capital. The thresholds are assumptions set against the 20-60% dealer price range and the 10-22% take rates above. Dealers appear to buy outright (researchers' inference from dealer websites), so step 1 also tests whether holders will wait for a consignment sale.

| Step | Ask | Pass | Kill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Call about 20 holders of surplus or decommissioned power circuit breakers and switchgear | Will you consign named units with spec sheets and an ask price? | At least 3 agree to consign | None. A miss fails the pass. |
| Call about 20 buyers: electrical contractors and data-center builders | Will you buy reconditioned gear with a 12-month warranty, and at what share of new price? | At least 3 will buy at 60% or more of new price | Buyers' specifications or inspectors require new equipment |
| Ask 2 reconditioning shops | Per-unit price and turnaround to recondition and re-mark under the shop's name | A price and turnaround from at least 1 shop | No shop will put its name on gear for a third party |
| Ask 1 insurer or warranty administrator | Quote for a 12-month warranty on reconditioned units | Reconditioning plus warranty cost leaves at least 15% of the sale price | None. A miss fails the pass. |
